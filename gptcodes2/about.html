Who We Are
From the Multiverse to Your Hands

DD Books started in 2019 as a small passion project — two friends with too many long boxes and not enough shelf space. What began as a weekend market stall quickly grew into Chennai's go-to destination for Spider-Man and Marvel comics.

We believe every great story deserves a great home. Whether you're hunting down a first appearance, filling gaps in a run, or just stepping into the Spider-Verse for the first time — we've got you covered.

Every comic we stock is hand-picked, carefully stored, and shipped with the love it deserves.
